Our Unforgettable Firsts

Looking back at how it all began

Milestone 01

First Dance

April 26, 2024

Ito ang pinakauna ng mga Firsts. Feeling ko kung hindi tayu naging partners sa cotillion walang mangyayare satin eh HAHAHAHAH. Pero seeing you in that elegant dark green dress, while holding your hand all throughout the dance, wala na pinish na, nabihag mo na ako HAHAHHAHA. I want to relive that moment, and have a small talk with you while dancing, at syempre isayaw ka na talaga nung ravee.

Milestone 02

First Date

June 3, 2024

Alala mo pa ata kung gaano tayu ka akward nung first official date natin, buti nalang marami tayung chika sa isa’t-isa para mawala man lamang kahit papaano yung hiya and awkwardness. Pero in that time, I knew that we had something, nag start man tayu as friends pero yun yung naging foundation natin para mapalalim pa relationship natin and go beyond friendship.

Milestone 03

First LDR

July 29, 2024

Itong picture yung last date natin before first time na ldr, and masyado pa tayo non avoidant that we will be apart for 5 months after that day. I still remember how we played roblox just to have one last time together, and how I hesitated so much just to tell you how I really felt about having a long distance relationship with you. Pero in those months, wala namang nagbago, though we had our uncertainties, we fought through it.

Milestone 04

First Monthsary

July 17, 2025

I still remember our simple celebration of our first monthsary, typical date lang natin na sine, photobooth, and kain. Pero the day itself made it special since we were celebrating our first monthsary, kita ko rin how your eyes were glowing like they always do. Itong araw den actually yung first ‘I love you’ natin sa isa’t-isa hehe, matagal ko na siyang gustong sabihin nonn, love you mwa

Milestone 05

First Anniversary

June 17, 2026

That first anniv date was indeed one of the best, and isa rin sa fave dates natin. Ang cute lang kase na chill picnic date na may movie date na sinamahan mo pa ng ganda ng mayon. We just enjoyed our time together as the sun sets down hanggang na dumilim yung paligid natin. Another is yung grab rides natin, keeping us safe papunta tas pabalik na may subtle physical touch moments natin hehe, kahit tahimik lang tayung magkahawak kamay at nakasandig ka saakin, I really enjoyed it. Thank you rin sa giftt!! Huhu yung bookmark lang na hindi mo pa magagamit tas ito lang naprepare ko, tig para isipan ko kung bibilhan kita ng necklacee, sayang bagay pa naman sa top mo noonn, I will make bawi. I love you, happy annivv
